## Scope

Homepage (`/`) of the rebuilt portfolio. Visitor mode: **Persuade** — a recruiter/hiring manager decides whether to move June forward.

## Audience, job, proof, constraints

- **Audience:** design hiring managers/recruiters, time-pressured, scanning.
- **Job:** get a fast, confident read on June's design skill and process, then act (contact, résumé, next step).
- **Proof/content:** bio + work list drawn from confirmed Evidence on Hand in `PRODUCT.md` (Manifest OS, Ghost AI placeholder, Onect AI, GEMI, XOMOX, plus Fun/Archive). No single flagship — projects shown at roughly equal weight (per owner's explicit answer), not ranked by a picked hero. Nav is Work + About only — Art is confirmed out of scope for this build.
- **Constraints:** Manifest OS is NDA'd — no real product screens. Ghost AI is genuinely unreleased — render as a clear "coming soon" state, not a filled-out case study. XOMOX has no detail page on the source site — title/year/role only, do not invent its narrative.

## Direction contract

**THESIS:** The homepage proves rigor through restraint, not through chrome — a single bio line and a plain, scannable list of real work stand in for the hero-image-plus-card-grid every competing portfolio ships.

**OWN-WORLD:** Preserved system per `DESIGN.md` (2026-09-15): flat white ground, Inter throughout (500 for everything that matters, 400 only for the year), warm-charcoal ink (#33302F) stepped through label/tag/year grays, tight negative letter-spacing, no accent color, no shadows, rounded treatment reserved for the circular avatar and the pill List/Grid toggle only.

**STORY:** A recruiter lands, reads the one-sentence bio under June's photo, scans the Work/About tabs, and scrolls a plain list of projects (title, year, role/tag) with equal visual weight — no single project is staged as the hero. Hovering a row previews the project. Contact (email/résumé/LinkedIn) stays reachable without hunting.

**FIRST VIEWPORT:** Avatar (top-left, circular, ~50px) above the one-line bio headline (largest text on the page); Work/About tabs plus the List/Grid pill toggle sit just below; the work list begins immediately under that — no hero image, no gradient, no card grid at rest.

**FORM:** Preserved incumbent identity, not a rolled direction — the user explicitly asked to keep the visual system from their live site (ycho.me) rather than take the dealt "Patent Drawing Plate" direction or any challenger from the 2026-09-15 direction round (seed key `2cf02048`). No new seed key applies; `DESIGN.md` is the authority.

**FINISH:** unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
